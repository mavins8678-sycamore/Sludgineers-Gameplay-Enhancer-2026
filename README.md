## 🎮 About the Game

Sludgineers is a single-player casual simulation and active incremental game developed by Snickerdoodle Games and Funk Games. It was released on August 25, 2026.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Sludgineers**. This trainer enhances Sludgineers by providing control over cash, resources, refinery processing, equipment upgrades, area progression, and power-up usage while you clean pollution, mine materials, complete quests, and automate production.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a relaxed progression experience with faster resource gathering, easier upgrades, accelerated refinery production, and convenient access to the game's areas and automation systems.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Move through the countryside using Sludgineers' standard controls. The trainer does not replace movement input, but its resource and upgrade options can make exploration and equipment improvement faster.

### Tools & Inventory

Cleaned pollution and mined materials provide resources used by the refinery and progression systems. Activate the resource multiplier before collecting materials, then use instant processing or unlimited cash when refining and upgrading.

### World & Environment

Explore the countryside, clean pollution from each area, complete quests, and unlock progressively larger and more complex levels. Enable the area unlock option only after saving your game and disable it before normal progression whenever possible.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no conventional combat in Sludgineers. Gameplay focuses on environmental cleanup, mining, resource refinement, equipment upgrades, power-ups, refinery automation, and drones.
